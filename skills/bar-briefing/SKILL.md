# Bar Briefing

## When to Use

- Reading the market.

## How It Works

Use the briefing; compute only what it doesn't hold. Without one, the news
blackouts are `blackouts_list`.

| Tool | Arguments | What it returns | Script (people) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bar_briefing` | `symbol` (a root, or a contract by month), `json`? | the bar briefing the autonomous runner built for the bar this cycle decides, opening with a plain-language summary (price and 1R, trend rule, alignment, regime, VWAP, news, the room above and below, a line per timeframe for its last completed candle, the latest CISD per timeframe, the next swing targets, each setup with its facts), times in ET, distances in points and R (1R is a setup's own stop, else the reference stop, 0.5 x ATR(20) of the briefing's own bars, named by its timeframe: `oneR`). `m3`, every bar: price, the bar (OHLC, range in ATR), ET time and the session window with its scope (`windowText`: market open or closed, entries allowed or not, until when; also `windowEntries`, `windowRefusal`, `marketOpen`; from the gate's own clock config), the next news blackout (`news`), regime (tags, metrics), EMA9/20/50/200, ADX, SuperTrend, Keltner, ATR(14), VWAP (which applies, side, distance, crosses), participation, the skip-rule counters (`context`), `referenceStop`, swings and liquidity (equal highs and lows marked taken or not), `lastBreak` (BOS or CHoCH with its age), `sweeps` (key levels first; only levels that existed before), `htfLevels` (the 15m and 1h swings against price, and the last one a 3m close broke), `untested1hSwings`, `nearestLiquidity` (the three nearest levels each side; `at`, the round number price sits on, only when it sits on one; `roundNumbers`, why there are none, only when the family's configured step was refused), today so far, the overnight range, the session profile and its nodes, `location`, `cisd` (the latest CISD per timeframe, 3m, 5m, 15m and 1h, on completed candles: its direction, level, displacement, any key-level sweep before it, and its leg's OTE zone with its status and whether price is in it; a read, not a signal), `candles` (per timeframe, 3m, 5m, 15m and 1h, the last COMPLETED candle, never the one forming: OHLCV, its close time, body and wick shares, close third, range in that timeframe's ATR, volume against its average, a pattern label (doji, hammer, shooting star, bullish or bearish engulfing, inside, outside, displacement, or none), and the key levels it met as `rejected` (traded through, closed back), `broke` (closed beyond, most of the body beyond) or `touched`, a fixed level as it stood when the candle opened (never one its own bars made, nor one formed later), a moving one (VWAP, the session profile) at its close, plus the round numbers in its range (on its family's step; none without one), its previous candle and the CRT range, at most four, strongest first (`moreLevels`: the rest); a timeframe that isn't whole base bars, 5m from 3m bars, is built from a 1m lane's bars when one runs (the candle ending at this close only once its last 1m bar is in), else `unavailable` with the reason (left out of the summary when no 1m lane runs), never approximated), `swingTargets` (the next swing high above and low below on the base bars, 15m and 1h, in points, ticks and R), `candidates` (each with its stop, exit, the first level in its way in R, the room it needs, and its context as `facts`: the trend rule and the multi-timeframe read for its side, room, confluence, VWAP side, window; `null`: the scan failed or the bar wasn't scanned; `blocked` in a no-entry window (named, with its end), a news blackout, or with the news unknown), `trendRule`. `m15`, until the next 15m close: `frame` (trend, bias, close, EMA20/50, swing pattern, ADX, ATR, and the last 20 candles' range with its mid), `alignment`, `lastBreak` (times are candle opens; `knownAt` is the breaking candle's close), `indicators` (EMA9/20, regime, ADX, SuperTrend, Keltner). `h1`, until the next 1h close: the 1h and 4h frames, `lastBreak`. `day`, until the first bar after 16:00 ET: the prior RTH day and its profile. Each part has a version and changes only on expiry. Reuse it; don't recompute what it holds. When it answers that there is no current briefing, or that it is old, read the bars yourself. With `timeframe` (`"15m"`, `"1h"`; only when the caller gave you one: the runner config puts some analysts on their own timeframe): that timeframe's briefing, `bar` (the `m3` facts on its own bars, valid until its next bar close, with a pointer to the runner's `candles` and `swingTargets` (the session's levels, one value each), the last bar before the 16:00 ET break until the first bar after the open; 1R and ATR named by its timeframe; no trend rule: the gate's current one is the runner's briefing's; no session facts (today, overnight, VWAP, session profile, `location`: `sessionLevels` points to the runner's briefing, their one value), so its levels and room are its own bars' (a lane's, whose setups are traded, add the runner's session levels, its values); setups only when a lane trades those bars, else in the runner's briefing) and the runner's parts that change no faster (`m15` up to 15m, `h1` up to 1h, `day`); its bars file is named in it. Your report on it may be reused until that close, so it may be up to one of its bars old | `briefing.js --symbol MNQ [--timeframe 15m] [--json]` |
| `bars_fetch` | `symbol`, `timeframe` (minutes 1-60, or `"daily"`), `count`?, `record`?, `name`? | writes closed bars to `<FTH_HOME>/bars-scratch/<SYMBOL>-<tf>m.json`, this harness home's own scratch folder (`-1d.json` daily; with `name`, `<SYMBOL>-<tf>m-<name>.json`, a file of your own) and returns that path, the contract, and when the last bar closed; `record` also records the multi-timeframe read | `bars.js --symbol MNQ --timeframe 3 [--daily] [--count N] [--record]` |
| `market_snapshot` | `bars`, `params`?, `symbol`? | the indicator and level snapshot (JSON, on one line; profile levels on the tick of `symbol`, default the root the bars file is named for) | `market-snapshot.js <bars> [--symbol=MNQ] [--name=value]` |
| `mtf_read` | `bars`, `timeframes`?, `daily`?, `json`?, `record`?, `symbol`? | the multi-timeframe read; `record` (with `symbol`) records it; in an autonomous cycle whose bar briefing is current, the default read instead points to it (its `m15` and `h1` hold the frames and the alignment, `m3` `trendRule` the trend rule); `daily`, other `timeframes` or `json` still read | `mtf.js <bars> [--tf=15,60,240] [--daily=<file>] [--json] [--record --symbol MNQ]` |
| `blackouts_list` | none | the news blackout windows | `blackouts.js list` |
| `blackouts_add` | `start`, `end` (ISO with a zone), `reason` | adds a window (append-only, at most 12 hours) | `blackouts.js add --start <ISO> --end <ISO> --reason <text>` |

## Examples

```text
bars_fetch {"symbol": "MNQ", "timeframe": 15, "count": 160}
→ wrote 160 15-minute bars for MNQ (tick 0.25, $0.5/tick) to /home/u/.futures-trading-harness/bars-scratch/MNQ-15m.json; last bar 2026-10-07T13:45:00.000Z, closed 2026-10-07T14:00:00.000Z

market_snapshot {"bars": "/home/u/.futures-trading-harness/bars-scratch/MNQ-15m.json"}
mtf_read {"bars": "/home/u/.futures-trading-harness/bars-scratch/MNQ-3m.json", "daily": "/home/u/.futures-trading-harness/bars-scratch/MNQ-1d.json"}
blackouts_add {"start": "2026-10-07T12:25:00Z", "end": "2026-10-07T12:40:00Z", "reason": "CPI 08:30 ET"}
```
